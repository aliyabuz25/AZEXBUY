## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the authority, discretion, and reliability of a premier national export facilitation and sourcing institution. It addresses institutional procurement officers, international trade partners, and enterprise buyers navigating cross-border trade, verification, and procurement pipelines.

The visual style is rooted in **Corporate Minimalism with Institutional Refinement**. Interfaces must communicate fiscal weight and diplomatic seriousness. The aesthetic rejects consumer tech frivolities: no exaggerated pill shapes, no iridescent gradients, no playful float animations, and no ambient multi-hue glows. Instead, visual authority is achieved through meticulous grid alignment, structural hairline borders, disciplined typographic hierarchy, and rich contrast between deep nautical tones and restrained bullion accents. Editorial and documentary-grade trade imagery (freight infrastructure, certified agricultural inspection, industrial manufacturing, secure dry ports) replaces generic corporate stock illustration.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated for institutional gravitas and international trade clarity:

- **Primary (`#102C46`)**: Sovereign Caspian Navy. Serves as the primary brand anchor, header surfaces, primary action buttons, key metrics, and dominant typography on light grounds.
- **Secondary (`#C4993D`)**: Refined Trade Gold. Deployed selectively for institutional badges, certified export indicators, active navigation marks, primary focal tags, and secondary action framing. Never used for massive flooded fills or loud marketing banners.
- **Tertiary (`#2B4C6F`)**: Slate Harbor. Provides subtle tonal distinction for secondary navigation bars, data visualization tracks, and elevated structural borders on dark surfaces.
- **Neutral Foundation**:
  - Canvas & Neutral Light: `#F8F9FA` (Paper Ground) and `#EBF0F5` (Tonal Container Fill).
  - Hairline Border: `#E2E8F0` for crisp data framing and table dividers.
  - Neutral Secondary Text: `#6C7A87` for metadata, export codes, HS code annotations, and subheadings.
  - Deep Ground Text: `#0A1C2C` for maximum contrast high-density text readability.

Color usage adheres to strict functional discipline: alert states use standard regulatory crimson (`#991B1B`) and compliance emerald (`#166534`), both desaturated to maintain an institutional posture.

## Typography
Typographic rules reflect structured corporate reporting and administrative clarity:

- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric exactness and modern stature. Headlines prioritize optical balance with tightened letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain an authoritative, dense editorial hierarchy.
- **Body & Data Density (`Inter`)**: Governs all transactional forms, trade agreements, supplier registries, and analytical ledgers. High x-height ensures immediate legibility in complex tabular layouts.
- **Label & Registry Identifiers (`label-caps`)**: Always uppercase with widened tracking (`0.08em`) for sovereign certificate tags, HS codes, VAT/TIN indicators, Incoterms abbreviations (FOB, CIF, DAP), and status indicators.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined, enterprise-grade grid architecture optimized for institutional workflows:

- **Desktop (1200px - 1440px+)**: A strict 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of `1360px`, framed by `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Administrative dashboards and catalog matrices leverage full-width fluid layouts with pinned utility rails.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: An 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-column catalog grids reflow to paired two-column structures.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: A 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Complex multi-step trade verification forms flatten into single-column flows.
- **Spatial Rhythm**: Internal component padding adheres to a compact, non-wasteful rhythm: inputs and buttons utilize `0.5rem` vertical and `1rem` horizontal padding; tabular data rows maintain a balanced `0.75rem` row clearance to maximize vertical scanability without visual congestion.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation eschews dramatic consumer drop shadows and glowing neomorphic planes in favor of **structural containment and low-contrast hairline boundaries**:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: `#F8F9FA` base application ground.
  - `Level 1 (Card / Ledger Surface)`: `#FFFFFF` resting on canvas, bordered by `#E2E8F0` (1px solid border). Zero shadow.
  - `Level 2 (Active / Hover / Overlay)`: `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a restrained, ambient institutional drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 44, 70, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(16, 44, 70, 0.04)`.
  - `Level 3 (Modals / Trade Dossiers)`: Raised white plane bound by a 1px solid border in `#102C46` (12% opacity) with a directional shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 44, 70, 0.12)`.
- **Dividers & Structural Rules**: Tables, header bars, and split columns utilize 1px solid `#E2E8F0` hairline rules. Avoid decorative gradient lines or heavy drop shadow separators.

## Shapes
Geometry is precise, architectural, and restrained:

- **Base Radii (`roundedness: 1`)**: Standard interactive elements (buttons, text inputs, search filters, check controls) feature an exact `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- **Container Radii**: Panels, cards, and data summaries scale to `8px` (`0.5rem`) maximum (`rounded-lg`). Over-rounded containers and bubbly curves are strictly forbidden to retain institutional solemnity.
- **Pill Exclusion**: Full-radius rounded pills are prohibited across core UI elements. Category tags and verification chips remain structured rectangles with micro-radii (2px to 4px).
